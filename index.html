FURPS+ (requirement type)

  • F Functionality: features, capabilities, behaviour.
  • U Usability: ease of use, accessibility, user experience.
  • R Reliability: availability, accuracy, recovery.
  • P Performance: response time, throughput, resource use.
  • S Supportability: maintainability, testability, adaptability.
  • + Constraints: design, implementation, interface, legal (e.g. GDPR), physical.

MoSCoW (priority)

  • Must Essential for the agreed solution or release.
  • Should Important, but a workable solution exists without it.
  • Could Desirable if time and resources permit.
  • Won't Explicitly excluded from this release (keep the rationale in Notes).

Security and other cross-cutting concerns may be classified under F or +. Choose one convention and apply it consistently.

Import: filformater
  • .csv: første række er overskrifter, de samme som i CSV-eksporten. Semikolon, komma og tab virker. Gem som "CSV UTF-8" i Excel, så æøå bevares.
  • .json: en liste af objekter, som i JSON-eksporten.
  • .txt: ét krav pr. linje. Alle får F / Should / Proposed, som du kan rette bagefter.

Import tilføjer nye krav og overskriver aldrig eksisterende. Kravene får nye ID'er, og et gammelt ID fra filen skrives i Notes. Importér ikke samme fil to gange, ellers får du dubletter. Tip: en CSV-eksport fra et tomt projekt er en færdig skabelon.
